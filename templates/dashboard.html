// under the hood

Isolation Forest:
The Algorithm Built for Anomalies

Core ML Algorithm
Isolation
Forest
Unlike clustering or density-based approaches, Isolation Forest works by isolating anomalies — abnormal observations are fewer in number and have distinct attribute values, making them easier to isolate.

It builds an ensemble of random trees, and anomalies are the data points that require fewer splits to isolate, yielding a continuous risk score from 0 to 1.

Unsupervised Detection
No labeled fraud data required. The model learns what "normal" looks like from your own transaction history, making it adaptive to each enterprise's unique patterns.

Real-Time Risk Scoring
Each transaction receives a continuous anomaly score (0–1). Scores above 0.75 trigger high-risk alerts. The closer to 1.0, the more isolated — and suspicious — the transaction.

Entity Behavioral Profiling
Beyond individual transactions, we aggregate risk signals across entities (senders & receivers), identifying high-risk counterparties across your entire transaction network.
📊
Systemic Risk Index
The overall anomaly rate across uploaded transactions gives your security team a single systemic risk score — instantly telling you whether your financial activity is unusual.
